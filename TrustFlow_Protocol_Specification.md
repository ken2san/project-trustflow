## 1. Abstract

TrustFlow is the world's first **AI-Native Escrow & Project Architecture Protocol**, designed to redefine freelance transactions through **Trust** and **Fluidity**.
By integrating a Neural Engine into the contract layer, it automates scoping, validation, and dispute resolution, creating a friction-free economic ecosystem.

---

## 2. Core Modules

### 2.1. 🧠 AI Neural Architect (Auto-Scoping Engine)

The Neural Architect module eliminates the ambiguity of project requirements.

**Output:** An immutable scope document, optimal budget estimation, and instant talent matching vectors.

**Inspection:** The AI code-scans deliverables against the signed DoD.

**Resolution:** Automatically proposes fair settlements (e.g., partial release, time extension) based on the calculated score, removing emotional bias from conflicts.

### 2.3. 🔄 Dynamic Scope Control (Adaptive Contracts)

A protocol for handling "Scope Creep" in real-time.
**Action:** Updates the smart contract and escrow vault balance dynamically upon biometric confirmation from both nodes.

## 3. Interface & UX Standards

- **Trigger:** Cmd+K (macOS) / Ctrl+K (Windows).
- **Capabilities:** Full system navigation, mode switching (Earner/Hirer), and wallet operations without cursor interaction.
- **Biometric Signature:** Simulates hardware-level fingerprint authentication for contract execution.
- **Haptic Confirmation:** "Hold-to-Confirm" interactions prevent accidental execution of high-value transactions.
